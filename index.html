Steps
  1. Book time with your AE to shadow 2 live discovery calls this week — one early-stage, one mid-funnel if possible.
  2. During each call, take structured notes: questions asked, prospect pain points surfaced, objections raised, next steps agreed.
  3. Within 24 hrs of each call, write a debrief (see template below).
  4. Share the debrief with your AE and ask for 10 min of feedback.
  5. Log your top 3 takeaways in your weekly self-reflection.
Debrief template
Call: [Prospect name] · [Date] AE: [Name] What the AE did well: - - What I'd do differently / add: - - Key pain points surfaced: - Economic buyer identified? Y / N — [Name/role] Biggest open question I'd ask next:
Done when
2 debriefs submitted to AE, at least 1 includes a concrete "I'd do differently" insight that your AE agrees is valid.

Steps
  1. Ask your AE for the 3 most common objections they hear at proposal/negotiation stage.
  2. Prepare your own response for each using the Acknowledge–Reframe–Validate structure (see template).
  3. Run the role-play: AE plays prospect, you handle each objection live.
  4. After each objection, pause and get real-time feedback before moving to the next.
  5. Repeat any objection you stumbled on until your AE rates your response 4+/5.
Response framework
Objection: "[Prospect's exact words]" A — Acknowledge: "That's a fair concern — a lot of our customers felt the same way..." R — Reframe: "What they found, though, was that [evidence / insight]..." V — Validate & advance: "Does that address it, or is there another piece we should dig into?" --- Common late-stage objections to prep: • "Your price is too high." • "We need to think about it / timing isn't right." • "We're already looking at [competitor]." • "We need sign-off from [person not in the room]."
Done when
All 3 objections role-played and AE scores each response 4+/5 — or you've repeated until reaching that bar. AE signs off in your scorecard.

Steps
  1. Identify a qualified prospect with your AE — ideally one you sourced yourself.
  2. Prepare a pre-call plan (see template) and share it with your AE 24 hrs before the call.
  3. Run the discovery call solo. AE joins silently — camera off or as a "colleague listening in."
  4. Within the same call or a follow-up, run the product demo tailored to pains uncovered.
  5. Send the follow-up email and next-step confirmation within 2 hrs of the call.
  6. Debrief with AE within 48 hrs — score yourself before they score you.

Steps
  1. At the start of each 2-week sprint, identify your most closeable deal — criteria: economic buyer engaged, pain confirmed, proposal sent or ready.
  2. Map the remaining steps to close using the closing checklist below.
  3. Drive every action independently — no AE rescue calls. If stuck, document the problem and bring it to your 1:1.
  4. When the deal closes (or stalls), document what happened and why.
  5. Share the closed deal story in the next team meeting.
Closing readiness checklist
Deal: [Company] □ Economic buyer confirmed and engaged □ Business case / ROI shared and acknowledged □ Proposal sent with clear pricing and scope □ Legal / procurement process understood □ Decision timeline confirmed □ Verbal commitment received □ Contract sent □ Signature / payment received Blockers to closing: 1. 2. My plan to remove each blocker: 1. 2.
Done when
At least 1 deal closed per 2-week sprint with zero AE involvement in the final stages. Manager verifies independence via CRM activity log.
Steps
  1. Choose a deal lost this month — ideally one that surprised you.
  2. Do a full timeline reconstruction from first touch to loss (calls, emails, CRM notes).
  3. Identify the root cause using the 5 Whys technique: ask "why?" 5 times until you hit the real reason.
  4. Build a 15-min presentation (no slides required — structured verbal is fine) using the retro template.
  5. Present to manager. Finish with 2–3 concrete changes you'll make on the next deal.

Steps
  1. Pair with one SDR — ideally someone earlier in tenure than you.
  2. Before the session, ask them: "What's one thing in prospecting you're struggling with right now?"
  3. Prepare a focused 10-min teaching piece on that topic (see example topics below).
  4. Run a 30-min live session: 10 min teach, 20 min practice/roleplay together.
  5. Send them a 3-bullet recap of what you covered and one thing to try that week.
  6. Check in the following week — did they try it? What happened?
Example session topics
Pick one per session based on their need: • Cold call opening lines that get 30 more seconds • How to write a 3-sentence email that books meetings • Building a call list — ICP signal vs. spray and pray • Handling "send me some info" brush-offs • How to leave a voicemail worth returning • Researching a prospect in under 5 minutes • Getting from gatekeeper to decision maker • How I think about opp quality before I book Session recap format (send via Slack/email): "Today we covered: [topic] Key insight: [1 sentence] Try this week: [specific action] Let me know how it goes!"
Done when
Session completed and recap sent. The SDR you coached can name one concrete thing they tried differently that week as a result.
